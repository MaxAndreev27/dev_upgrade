# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone who participates in this project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and give feedback about ideas, not people.
- Welcome different perspectives and experience levels.
- Report unacceptable behavior privately and promptly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or project work.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate this Code of Conduct. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

## Reporting

Report concerns by opening a private security advisory or contacting the repository owner. Reports will be handled respectfully and confidentially where possible.
